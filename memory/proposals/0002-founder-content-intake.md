## Why
The founders will return edited versions of content/approved-answers.md, qualifying-questions.md and handoff-rules.md, now and after launch. Each time someone must diff the changes, check no figures or dashes crept in, list remaining FOUNDER TO CONFIRM markers, update the spec if a rule changed, log it, and redeploy. Easy to forget a step.

## What
A project skill `founder-content-intake`: a checklist plus the existing lint hook, producing a short "what changed and what still needs a founder" note for Ioseb.
