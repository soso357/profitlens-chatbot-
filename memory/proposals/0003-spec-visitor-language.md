## Why
Many independent US restaurant owners write in Spanish. The spec and the approved answers are English only, and nothing says whether the agent should reply in the visitor's language (translating approved answers risks changing their meaning, rule R1) or reply in English and offer a founder.

## What
A founder decision, then a new behaviour row in docs/spec.md (B13) and two scripted test conversations.
