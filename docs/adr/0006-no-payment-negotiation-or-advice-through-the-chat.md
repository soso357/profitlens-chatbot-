# ADR 0006: No payment, negotiation or advice through the chat

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-22
- Decided by: Founders; payment point confirmed by Ioseb 2026-09-22

## Context

Payment and pricing need human judgment and carry risk.

## Decision

The chatbot never takes payment, never sends payment links or asks for card details, never negotiates and never gives advice. These topics go to a founder (spec R14, handoff rules).

## Consequences

No payment provider integration is ever built.
