# ADR 0031: Skip the weekly digest email

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-06
- Decided by: Ioseb (option 2, skip)
- Links: Phase 5 plan item 7; spec section 6 (integrations)

## Context

The founders' brief asked for a weekly email digest of transcripts so founders can read every conversation in the first month live. Since ADR 0019, every chat message already goes to the founders' Telegram group as it happens, and lead, booking and handoff emails keep the record.
Options:
1. Build it: one tidy weekly email with every chat and lead. Easy to read in one sitting; one more session of work and one more email job to keep working.
2. Skip it: founders already see every message in Telegram. No work, no new moving part; reading a week of chats means scrolling Telegram.
3. Ask the founders: they are the readers. Right people decide; Phase 5 waits for their answer.

## Decision

Skip the weekly digest. Founders review conversations in the Telegram group (ADR 0019) and the email alerts.

## Consequences

Phase 5 has no build items left. No extra cost. If the founders later want a weekly summary, it can be added as a new task; leads.csv and the logs on the Render disk already hold what it would need.
