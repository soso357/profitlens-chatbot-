## What is ProfitLens?

ProfitLens works out what each dish on your menu actually costs you. You send us your supplier invoices and recipes, and we calculate the food cost and profit per plate (what you keep after the ingredients are paid for) for every dish. You do not need spreadsheets or software. We do the work for you.

## Who is it for?

Independent restaurants with one to three locations, where the owner sets the menu prices, for example casual dining and fast casual restaurants.

## Is it done by a person or by software?

Every report is done and checked by a real person on our team, not generated by software on its own.

## What does it cost?

There are two options, both a one time payment with no subscription:
1. Food Cost Analysis: $99.
2. Menu Analysis Report: $149.
If you start with the $99 option and want the full report later, the upgrade is $50, so you never pay more than $149 in total. These are introductory prices for our first clients. The agent does not say when introductory pricing ends.

## What do I get with the Food Cost Analysis ($99)?

1. The food cost of every dish on your menu, calculated for you.
2. The profit per plate on each dish and your overall food cost.
3. An Excel workbook with every dish costed, yours to keep.
4. A short PDF summary in plain language showing where you stand.
It needs one 15 to 20 minute call and no forms.

## What do I get with the Menu Analysis Report ($149)?

Everything in the Food Cost Analysis, plus your sales from last month, so you can see which dishes make money, which do not, and what to change first. That includes:
1. Every dish placed on a menu matrix, which sorts dishes into four groups (Stars, Plowhorses, Puzzles and Dogs) by how popular and how profitable they are.
2. Which dishes earn the most, and which are taking up space on your menu.
3. Price suggestions that fit your own pricing style.
4. Kitchen labor cost per dish, from your real payroll.
5. A five point action plan for the week.

## What is the difference between the two options?

The Food Cost Analysis tells you what each dish costs and what you keep on it. The Menu Analysis Report adds your sales, so it also shows which dishes are worth keeping, which to change, and what to do first.

## What do I need to send?

1. Photos or PDFs of your last 4 weeks of supplier invoices.
2. Any recipe notes you have.
3. A photo of your menu.
For the Menu Analysis Report, also send last month's sales export from your POS (your till or point of sale system).
Any format works, and there are no forms to fill in. The agent never mentions an Excel template or a form.

## What happens on the call?

It is one short call of 15 to 20 minutes with one of our founders. We go through your recipes together and fill in any gaps from your invoices. You do not need to prepare spreadsheets. It is a video call on Google Meet, and a founder always takes it.

## How long does it take?

Your results arrive within 5 business days of the call.

## Why did you build it?

Big restaurant chains have teams of analysts making sure their menus make money. Independent restaurants do not. We built ProfitLens to give independent owners the same clarity, explained in plain English.

## What happens to my data?
